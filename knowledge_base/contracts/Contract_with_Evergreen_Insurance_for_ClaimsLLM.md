# Contract: Evergreen Insurance – ClaimsLLM

Insurellm has partnered with Evergreen Insurance to deploy ClaimsLLM
for intelligent claims processing and analysis.

## Scope of Agreement
- Automated analysis of claim descriptions
- AI-assisted claim triaging
- Detection of inconsistencies in submitted claims

## Purpose
This partnership aims to reduce claim processing time and improve
decision accuracy using AI-driven insights.
