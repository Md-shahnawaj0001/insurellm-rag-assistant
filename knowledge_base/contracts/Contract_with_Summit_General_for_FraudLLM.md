# Contract: Summit General – FraudLLM

Insurellm has partnered with Summit General to deploy FraudLLM for
AI-driven fraud detection in insurance claims.

## Scope of Agreement
- Detection of suspicious claim patterns
- Natural language analysis of claim descriptions
- Risk scoring for manual investigation prioritization

## Purpose
The goal of this partnership is to reduce fraudulent claims and improve
overall claims processing efficiency.

## Status
Active partnership
