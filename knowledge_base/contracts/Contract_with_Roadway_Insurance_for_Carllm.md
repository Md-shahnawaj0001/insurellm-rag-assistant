# Contract: Roadway Insurance – Carllm

Insurellm has entered into a strategic partnership with Roadway Insurance
to deploy the Carllm product for auto insurance operations.

## Scope of Agreement
- Use of Carllm for AI-driven risk assessment
- Automated underwriting support
- Fraud detection for auto insurance policies

## Purpose
The objective of this contract is to improve underwriting accuracy and
operational efficiency for Roadway Insurance using AI-powered solutions.

## Status
Active partnership
