# About Insurellm

Insurellm is an InsurTech company focused on building AI-driven solutions for the
insurance and reinsurance industry.

Founded in 2015, the company aims to simplify complex insurance workflows using
large language models (LLMs), machine learning, and data-driven systems.

Insurellm works with insurers to improve underwriting, risk assessment,
policy management, and operational efficiency through intelligent automation.
