# Contract: Pioneer Insurance – UnderwriteLLM

Insurellm has entered into an agreement with Pioneer Insurance to use
UnderwriteLLM for AI-assisted underwriting decisions.

## Scope of Agreement
- Risk profiling of insurance applicants
- Support for underwriting recommendations
- Data-driven premium estimation

## Purpose
The objective of this contract is to improve underwriting consistency
and reduce manual decision-making through AI.

## Status
Active partnership
