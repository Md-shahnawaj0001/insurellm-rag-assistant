# Contract: Apex Reinsurance – ReLLM

Insurellm has partnered with Apex Reinsurance to deploy the ReLLM platform
for reinsurance risk analysis and portfolio evaluation.

## Scope of Agreement
- Large-scale risk modeling for reinsurance portfolios
- AI-assisted treaty analysis
- Scenario simulation using historical loss data

## Purpose
The partnership aims to help Apex Reinsurance make data-driven decisions
for risk transfer and capital allocation.

## Status
Active partnership
